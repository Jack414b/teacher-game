Use the Baum–W elch algorithm, which is the expectation-maximization (EM) algorithm specialized
to HMMs. The latent state paths are unobserved, and enumerating all 2 20 paths for each sequence is

expected numbers of transitions s1 → s1, s1 → s2, s2 → s1, and s2 → s2. Sum these expected counts
