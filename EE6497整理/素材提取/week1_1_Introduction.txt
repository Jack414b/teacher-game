Probability Review
Probabilistic Perspective
What is a random variableX?
▶ X gives random values with certain probabilities. p X ( x )is the “chance” or our
“belief” thatX=x.
▶ Depending on whetherXis discrete or continuous,p X (x)is called the
probability mass function (pmf) or probability density function (pdf).
▶ In this course, we always assume either discrete random variable (pmf) or
continuous random variable (pdf). In our discussion, we will just talk about pdf.
Supervised learning: outputYmay depend on factors other than givenX.
Treat the other factors as “random” effects. Model input and output as a pair
of random elements(X,Y)with joint pdfp X,Y (x,y).
Unsupervised learning: dataXis generated by a “random” process. Model
data as a random elementXwith pdfp X (x).

Probability Review
Parametric vs Non-parametric Models
Parametric model: p ( x,y ) = p ( x,y|θ )or p ( x ) = p ( x|θ )has a fixed number
of parameters, usually belongs to a fixed family of distributions.
▶ Faster to train (find “optimal”θ).
▶ Stronger assumptions about the data distribution
Non-parametric model: number of parameters grow with the amount of
training data.
▶ More flexible
▶ Can be computationally intractable for large datasets

If p ( θ)and p ( θ|x )have the same form (belongs to same distribution family),
we say that they areconjugate distributions, andp(θ)is aconjugate priorfor
